# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- The Corgi layout: one Herdr window with Go to, PR review, the chat bar, dictation and the time tracker.
- The corgi CLI: enable, disable, status, update, rollback, diff, resolve, dev, save, release, bundle, sync, doctor and config.
- Updates wait a few days after a release before they are offered (minimum release age), with an opt-in for early updates.
- Local edits to a mod survive updates through a three-way merge.
- The updater mod, the shared conventions and the interplay test.
